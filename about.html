The Founder

Meet Sege Peter ENG

Founder & CEO | Software Engineer | Creative Technologist | Digital Innovation Strategist

Sege Peter ENG is a visionary Software Engineer, Creative Technologist, and entrepreneur dedicated to building technology that transforms businesses and communities. As the Founder and CEO of Skope Designs, he leads the development of innovative software solutions, enterprise systems, and digital products that help organizations automate operations, improve efficiency, and accelerate growth.

With a strong foundation in software engineering, UI/UX design, branding, and digital strategy, Sege bridges creativity with technology to deliver intelligent solutions that solve real-world business challenges. His expertise extends beyond creating visually appealing designs—he architects secure, scalable, and high-performance digital ecosystems that empower organizations to thrive in an increasingly digital world.

Over the years, Sege has successfully designed and developed a diverse portfolio of solutions, including School Management Systems (SMS), Learning Management Systems (LMS), Hospital Management Information Systems (HMIS), Human Resource Management Systems (HRMS), Point of Sale (POS) systems, Hotel Management Systems (HMS), Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platforms, Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems, NGO management platforms, e-commerce solutions, and other custom business applications. Each solution is carefully engineered to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and deliver measurable business value.

His technical expertise spans modern software development frameworks and technologies, including React, Next.js, Laravel, PHP, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Firebase, REST APIs, cloud technologies, and business intelligence tools such as Power BI. He is also experienced in integrating secure payment systems, third-party APIs, cloud infrastructure, and AI-powered automation into enterprise applications.

Beyond software development, Sege is an accomplished brand strategist and UI/UX designer who has helped businesses, startups, NGOs, educational institutions, healthcare organizations, and public sector institutions establish compelling digital identities and engaging user experiences. His approach combines strategic thinking, human-centered design, and robust engineering to create solutions that are both aesthetically refined and technically exceptional.

As a technology leader, Sege is passionate about digital transformation, innovation, and empowering organizations through technology. He believes that software should not simply automate tasks—it should create opportunities, simplify complexity, and enable sustainable growth. Through Skope Designs, he continues to champion the development of future-ready digital solutions that drive innovation across Africa and beyond.

"I believe technology should do more than solve problems—it should create opportunities, inspire innovation, and transform the way businesses and communities grow." — Sege Peter ENG

Skope Designs is a Digital Innovation & Software Development Studio that helps businesses, institutions, and organizations transform ideas into powerful digital solutions.
